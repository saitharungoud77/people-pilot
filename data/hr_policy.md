## Leave

- There are six leave types. In a full calendar year an employee is entitled to
  12 General Leave days, 3 Sick Leave days, 2 Optional Leave days, 7 Bereavement Leave days,
  and Paternity Leave (7 days for male employees, 180 days for female employees).
- Loss of Pay is not an entitlement. It is used when there is no paid
  leave left, and any day taken as Loss of Pay reduces that month's pay.
- Leave is counted in working days only. Weekends and public holidays
  inside a leave range are free - Friday to Monday is two days, not four.
- A half day is available for paid leave and only for a single date.
  Loss of Pay is always taken in full days.
- A request is checked before it is saved: the balance has to cover it,
  the range cannot clash with leave already approved or pending, and a
  range made up entirely of weekends, holidays or past days is refused.
- Leave balances count only leave that HR has approved. Days applied for
  but not yet approved do not reduce the balance.

## Payslips

- One payslip is created for every employee each month, dated the 1st of
  the pay month.
- A payslip shows gross pay, deductions, provident fund, loss-of-pay days
  and net pay, and can be downloaded as a PDF.

## Salary

- An employee's salary is built from their Annual CTC. The portal splits
  the CTC into basic pay, HRA, special allowance and any other allowance,
  and works out the statutory provident fund from that.
- When the CTC is revised, the whole monthly structure is recalculated so
  the components stay in proportion.

## Provident fund

- The employee contributes 12% of basic pay. The employer also
  contributes 12% of basic pay, capped at ₹25,000 per month, split into
  3.67% employer EPF and 8.33% EPS pension. The employee's 12% EPF
  contribution is subject to the same configured wage cap.
- The PF balance shown is the running total of contributions only. No
  interest is added. EPS pension is displayed separately and is not
  included in the EPF balance.
- An employee's PF statement starts from their joining month.

## Attendance and holidays

- Attendance is recorded one day at a time by HR.
- Holidays are company-wide. Only the current year's holiday list is
  maintained in the portal.

## Full and final settlement

- When an employee leaves, a full and final settlement is calculated from
  their exit date, including any earned leave balance and gratuity where
  it applies.

## POSH policy

- AIT Solutions follows the Prevention of Sexual Harassment of Women at
  Workplace Act, 2013. Sexual harassment is not tolerated, in any form,
  by anyone.
- The full POSH policy - what counts as harassment, how to complain, the
  Internal Committee and the timelines - is on the "POSH policy" page in
  the portal, and in the file data/posh_policy.md.
- A complaint is made in writing to the Internal Committee, at
  posh@aitsolutions.com or to HR, within three months of the incident.
  The committee finishes its inquiry within ninety days.
- Ask the employee to open the POSH policy page for the complete wording.
  Do not summarise legal detail beyond what is written in this file.
